# Provisional cognitive-demand rubric

Defined before the expansion is authored. This is an authoring rubric, not a psychometrically validated scale. Existing questions and their difficulty values are preserved.

| Level | Reasoning tag | Required demand |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | recognition | Identify one explicitly cued fact, structure, mechanism or calculation input. |
| 2 | application | Apply one principle to a novel vignette or perform one interpreted calculation. |
| 3 | integration | Combine at least two independent findings or infer an intermediate mechanism before answering. |
| 4 | prioritization | Use several findings to distinguish plausible alternatives or prioritize a next step with a stated clinical constraint. |
| 5 | synthesis | Resolve competing diagnoses/management priorities across multiple constraints, explain why the closest alternatives fail, and acknowledge a relevant uncertainty or trade-off. |

Every expansion specification must include a reasoning tag and a concrete `difficultyReason` describing the operation the learner must perform. The numeric difficulty must equal the tag's rubric level. Longer stems, rare trivia, and arbitrary assignment are not grounds for a higher level. Automated checks validate the mapping and completeness; a substantive content review checks whether the claimed reasoning actually appears. Disputed ratings are flagged for review rather than silently increased.

Each 100-item batch should include more than one cognitive level and both foundational applications and integrated reasoning where appropriate to the discipline. No exact percentage is forced at the expense of suitable content. Empirical difficulty/discrimination must wait for sufficient learner data and appropriate analysis.
