# Name and logo policy

OpenPrep software currently remains under MIT; AGPL-3.0-only is the proposed transition, pending ownership/contributor review. Neither a software-license transition nor this policy establishes ownership or registration of a mark. OpenPrep name/logo rights, if any, remain with their actual holder. No registered trademark status or ownership by Ahmed Umer/Aqvion Labs is claimed by this audit.

Trademark permissions are separate from copyright permissions for logo source or artwork. The software license does not itself grant trademark rights. This policy does not withdraw any copyright permission already granted or add restrictions to an AGPL grant. Accurate descriptive references to OpenPrep and legally permitted nominative/fair use remain subject to applicable law.

Forks can meet their applicable software-license obligations while using their own product name and branding (`NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_NAME` configures the displayed name). Identify an independent fork honestly, link to its source, and avoid implying official maintenance, sponsorship or endorsement. Do not falsely represent an independent deployment as the official project.

Maintainer action required before a formal trademark permission program: establish actual ownership of the name/logo and asset rights, approve an authorized contact, and review any proposed branding guidelines. This document makes no claim of legal clearance or exclusivity.
