# Step 3 authoring standard

Use independently authored synthetic scenarios. No UWorld, official sample questions or proprietary question wording may be copied, paraphrased or reconstructed. Public outlines supply categories only. References support factual study; they do not authorize adapting protected wording.

Each `Step3Spec` records a stable `op-s3-0001` style ID, primary blueprint category, existing subject/system, topic/subtopic, unique tested concept, physician task, clinical setting, service line, nutrition flag, difficulty rationale, vignette, four/five choices with a rationale for **every** choice, explanation, objective, takeaways, factual references, evidence limitations and unresolved concerns. The first authored choice is the intended answer; deterministic option rotation changes only display order and marks the key. It never creates medical facts. IDs remain stable after import.

Minimum editorial bar: developed vignette with decisive timing, stability, relevant findings and contraindications; one focused decision; substantive explanation connecting those findings to the answer and competing choices. Avoid generic option rationales, answer-length cues, rare-fact trivia, unnecessary demographic details and unsupported absolute rules. Include units and thresholds explicitly. Do not present an outdated recommendation as current; identify jurisdiction where legal rules vary.

Difficulty is a provisional cognitive-demand rating, not measured learner performance: level2 applies a stated rule to a straightforward clinical decision; level3 integrates at least two findings or distinguishes plausible alternatives; level4 integrates temporal change, comorbidity, competing risks or sequential management. Level1 isolated recall is outside this Step3 collection. Level5 requires exceptional documented synthesis and is withheld absent an explicit rationale. Numeric calculations do not automatically justify higher difficulty. Validate the rating against its rationale and preserve uncertainty for review.

References must have real labels and HTTP(S) URLs, with access status and dated limitations recorded. Prefer official US guidelines, CDC/NIH/FDA/USPSTF, professional-society guidelines and peer-reviewed primary research. Distinguish source-page access from exact-claim support and from qualified clinical review. Neither a working URL nor AI critique establishes accuracy. Book-level or incomplete references remain flagged. A reviewer must verify management currency, dose/units, contraindications, answer uniqueness and every distractor before clinical approval.

Author in ten100-item batches under the weighted blueprint. Run structure, taxonomy, key, normalized exact/trigram, repeated objective/concept, semantic candidate, unsafe markup/media, unit/numeric and reference screens. Ambiguous or potential duplicates retain review findings. Structural acceptance permits **DRAFT/REVIEW import only**; it does not authorize learner delivery or export.

Current source's content-rights checks are mandatory. New items retain AI-authored/medically-unreviewed provenance, UNVERIFIED rights, false delivery/export grants and no reviewer identities. The claimed dataset license is the existing CC-BY-SA-4.0 convention, pending actual rightsholder/evidence review; it is not a verified permission. Root software license is unchanged. Human approval and practice-ready counts start at zero.

Development and tests use a newly named loopback disposable database. Never run these scripts against hosted databases, existing study databases or the configured shared test database. Preserve existing questions/options and all study data. Batch withdrawal may only archive owned unreferenced Step3 candidate IDs; it must not delete unrelated records. No commits, pushes, staging/production deployment or public content distribution are authorized.
