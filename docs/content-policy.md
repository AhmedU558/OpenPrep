# Content and licensing policy

## Licenses
- **Code** is MIT.
- **Bundled demo questions** (`legacy/questions.js`, `prisma/seed-data/`) are original works by OpenPrep contributors, licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
- **Every question stores its own `source`, `license` and optional `attribution`**, shown under each explanation. Content you add keeps its own license. MIT does not apply to it.

Why MIT for the code: all runtime dependencies are permissive (MIT, Apache-2.0, MIT-0, BSD-2-Clause for dotenv in dev), so MIT adds no conflicts and allows self-hosting and reuse. Content uses Creative Commons because it is prose, not software. ShareAlike keeps community-improved questions open.

## What may be added
- ✅ Questions you wrote yourself.
- ✅ Content under an open license that permits redistribution (e.g. CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0), with attribution recorded.
- ❌ Questions, explanations, images or diagrams copied or paraphrased from commercial question banks, review books, or other copyrighted sources.
- ❌ Invented references, statistics or guidelines. If you cannot cite a source you checked, leave references empty.

## Medical content
The expanded original bank is explicitly labeled AI-authored and medically unreviewed. Its availability does not imply qualified review. See [question-content-policy.md](question-content-policy.md) for provenance, reference limitations and the review workflow.
Demo content is labelled **demo** in the UI and is illustrative, not medical advice. Guidelines change, so an editor should review questions periodically. Learners can report problems with **Report an error**, and those reports go to the admin queue.

## Takedown
If you believe content infringes your rights, open an issue or follow `SECURITY.md` for private contact. Admins can archive a question immediately; archived questions disappear from practice.
