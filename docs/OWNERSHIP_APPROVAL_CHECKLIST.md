**Prospective software distribution:** “For [specified version/commit and original contributions], I authorize the proposed AGPL-3.0-only distribution on the reviewed scope and notices, subject to existing upstream license obligations. This authorization does not revoke prior MIT grants, transfer another person's copyright, license educational content, or grant trademark rights. Restrictions and exceptions are [list].
